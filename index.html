NPS vs OPS: Lifetime Retirement Simulator

Browser-based Monte Carlo model · Indian Central Government OPS proxy · all amounts in INR

Model, not an official pension calculator. The OPS pension is estimated as 50% of final pensionable basic pay, with an assumed annual pension/DR increase. Actual eligibility, emoluments, DR orders, commutation, gratuity, family pension and scheme-specific rules must be verified for the employee. The model excludes taxes and does not forecast official DR decisions.

Methodology & limitations

  • Central Government OPS proxy: initial basic pension = 50% of final pensionable pay, assuming qualifying-service eligibility. Actual CCS pension entitlement depends on applicable rules and the official emoluments/average-emoluments calculation.
  • DR is not mechanically forecast by government notifications. “Annual OPS pension/DR growth” is a simplified assumption applied to the total pension stream, not an official DR formula.
  • NPS accumulation uses annual lognormal returns calibrated so the expected one-year gross return is approximately 1 + the selected expected return. Contributions are approximated annually and grow with pay.
  • GPF is shown as a separate accumulated asset alongside OPS pension; it is not itself the OPS pension. GPF contributions are approximated as annual deposits.
  • Lifetime present value discounts nominal cash flows using a nominal rate implied by the chosen real discount rate and inflation. Inflation-adjusted pension chart is shown in today's rupees. Results depend strongly on assumptions.
  • Not modelled: taxes, pension commutation, gratuity, leave encashment, family pension, survivor-specific annuity pricing, salary/DA split, pay commissions, contribution timing, fees, and correlated market/inflation regimes.
